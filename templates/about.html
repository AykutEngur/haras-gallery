Γεια σας! Hello!

Hara Giannopoulos

I'm a Greek-American person with a strong passion for art, and Greece is at the heart of everything I do. The blue of the sea, the white of the island houses, the warmth of family tables full of fruit: these are the colors and moments I keep coming back to on paper.

I graduated from Binghamton University with my bachelor's degree magna cum laude. During my time there I served as President of the Binghamton Hellenic Society, bringing students together to celebrate Greek culture, traditions and community.

Drawing has always been my way of slowing down and noticing things. This gallery is where I share that work, and where you can ask me to create something personal just for you.

  • 🎓 B.A., Binghamton University, magna cum laude
  • 🇬🇷 Former President, Binghamton Hellenic Society
